# Privacy Policy — Upcoming for Toddle

_Last updated: September 28, 2026_

Upcoming for Toddle ("the extension") is a browser extension that shows a
student's upcoming Toddle assignments and deadlines. It is an independent
project and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Toddle.

## The short version

- The extension only talks to **Toddle's own servers**. It has no servers of
  its own, no analytics and no advertising, and it sends your data nowhere else.
- Your task list is stored **only on your computer**, in the extension's local
  browser storage.
- Nothing is sold, shared or used for anything except showing you your tasks.

## What the extension accesses

| Data | Why | Where it goes |
|---|---|---|
| Your Toddle login session (the token Toddle keeps in your browser for web.toddleapp.com) | To ask Toddle for your to-do list as you, the way Toddle's website does | Sent only to Toddle's API (`*.toddleapp.com` / `*.toddleapp.cn`) over HTTPS. It is used in memory and is never written to storage. |
| Your to-do list: assignment titles, class names, due dates, submission status, unread-message counts | To display them in the popup, the badge and the on-page panel | Stored locally in `chrome.storage.local` on your device |
| Your Toddle student, school, curriculum-program and academic-year IDs | To request the right to-do list and build links to your assignments | Stored locally in `chrome.storage.local` on your device |
| One setting (whether to show the "Upcoming" tab on Toddle pages) | Your preference | Stored locally |

The extension does **not** read your browsing history, other websites, your
email or the contents of your submissions. It does not collect your name or
email address.

## How long data is kept

The cached task list is replaced on every refresh (about every 30 minutes).
Everything is deleted when you remove the extension.

## Sharing

The extension does not sell, rent, transfer or share your data with anyone.
Data is exchanged only between your browser and Toddle, which is the service
you already use. Toddle's handling of your data is covered by Toddle's own
privacy policy.

## Children and students

The extension is designed for students. It collects no data beyond what is
listed above and sends nothing to anyone except Toddle.
